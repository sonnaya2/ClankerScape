Current route

Desert → Asgarnia → Anachronia

Necromancy first. Magic comes online after Crystal Grace. Keep skilling and PvM moving together instead of camping one lane.

A0–50

Misthalin + Havenhythe

bootstrap

Sweep dense low-requirement tasks. Push broad early skills, City of Um and cheap first-kill combat; stop long grinds before the next relic or XP multiplier.

Skill Thieving · Woodcutting · Firemaking · Fletching · Fishing · Cooking · Mining · Smithing
Fight early Necromancy · low-risk first kills · useful Slayer progress
B50–175

Karamja

one sweep

Karamja opens automatically at 50 tasks. Clear the cheap local cluster in one pass, then take the Fight Cave when the setup is already good enough.

Sweep seaweed → pineapple → banana crate → karambwanji → Brimhaven dungeon → stepping stones → arena ticket → snake → TzHaar → 25 lobsters
Power Jad + equip Fire cape when the first clear is reliable
C175–300

Desert

first elective

Take Desert at 175. Clear short Desert/global clusters and use Menaphos, production and combat unlocks to raise account capability without getting trapped in isolated grinds.

Skill batch nearby thresholds and production tasks while travel is local
Fight Necromancy remains the default route style; take first kills before repeat-kill milestones
D300–450

Asgarnia

infrastructure

Take Asgarnia at 300. Turn on Invention and Smithing infrastructure, then alternate short production batches with God Wars and mastery clusters.

Skill Smithing batches · Invention levels/items · process production stacks · Desert leftovers during downtime
Fight Graardor first → easy mastery cluster → Nex only when Defence and gear already make it cheap
E450–48K

Anachronia

point push

Take Anachronia at 450. Task-count speed no longer matters: finish on the best short 80/200-point clusters across Desert, Asgarnia and Anachronia.

Prefer first kills · short mastery steps · BGH/Slayer overlap · production batches · high-value skilling thresholds
Hold about 4,000 easy reserve points until the Dragon finish is locked
