Karamja

one sweep

Karamja opens automatically at 50 tasks. Clear the cheap local cluster in one pass, then take the Fight Cave when the setup is already good enough.

Sweepseaweed → pineapple → banana crate → karambwanji → Brimhaven dungeon → stepping stones → arena ticket → snake → TzHaar → 25 lobsters
PowerJad + equip Fire cape when the first clear is reliable

Desert

first elective

Take Desert at 175. Clear short Desert/global clusters and use Menaphos, production and combat unlocks to raise account capability without getting trapped in isolated grinds.

Skillbatch nearby thresholds and production tasks while travel is local
FightNecromancy remains the default route style; take first kills before repeat-kill milestones
D300–450

Asgarnia

infrastructure

Take Asgarnia at 300. Turn on Invention and Smithing infrastructure, then alternate short production batches with God Wars and mastery clusters.

SkillSmithing batches · Invention levels/items · process production stacks · Desert leftovers during downtime
FightGraardor first → easy mastery cluster → Nex only when Defence and gear already make it cheap

Take Anachronia at 450. Task-count speed no longer matters: finish on the best short 80/200-point clusters across Desert, Asgarnia and Anachronia.
