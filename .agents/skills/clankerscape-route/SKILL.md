# ClankerScape route planning

## Objective

Minimise expected wall-clock time to 48,000 points while preserving executable skilling and PvM alternatives. Stop at Dragon. Never optimise one combat dummy, one rare drop, one activity family, or theoretical regional points in place of the route.

## Authority

1. Current official Jagex League rules/reveals.
2. Current canonical RuneScape Wiki Equilibrium rows/mechanics.
3. Provenanced repository snapshots that agree with stronger sources.
4. Combat benchmarks only when source-clean and compatible.

Do not use Catalyst rows, another player's route, social commentary, remembered tasks, code comments, or passing tests as source authority. Unknown remains unknown.

## Source gate

A frozen route requires exactly 1,152 unique numeric task IDs and 109,380 points across valid 10/30/80/200/400-point tiers. Partial rows may be executed but missing coverage stays visible. Every newly sourced row begins `unclassified`; it contributes to no queue or reserve until reviewed. Source facts, route assumptions, local player state, and decisions remain separate.

## Portfolio

After classification maintain exactly five banks:

1. immediate skilling;
2. deep skilling;
3. immediate PvM;
4. deep PvM;
5. reserve.

Parked/blocked tasks are not executable bank coverage. Keep both next queues honest and include switching, setup, travel, supply, failure, crowding, waiting, P50, and P90 costs. A 4,000-point reserve must reconcile to real feasible unfinished rows.

Before 450 tasks, prioritise the 50/175/300/450 task gates, then profitable Blessing/relic breakpoints and prerequisites. After 450, prioritise deterministic points now, reliable points/minute, cluster value, optionality, and lower P90 risk.

## Regions

Never carry a region favourite from prose. At 175/300/450 compare every legal elective package using reachable rows, deterministic floor, skilling/PvM split, Blessing tasks, quest autocomplete, setup/travel, dependencies, supplies, P50/P90, and invalidation conditions. Published totals are only a feasibility ceiling. Overlapping ranges are `close`.

## Relics

Assume one permanent pick per tier. No ordinary relic reset is published. Rejuvenated adds one complete missed Tier 1–5 relic; it does not refund, replace, or reopen tiers.

At every tier score every choice by remaining-route minutes. Do not narrow Animal Wrangler to Fishing, Antiquarian to convenience, Devout to banking, or Perkfection to DPS.

Tier 6 requires:

- a minute estimate for every plausible missed Tier 1–5 relic;
- Perkfection direct savings from setup/materials, machines, charge, travel/access, and augmented combat;
- `20% more frequent helpful perks` applied only to the throughput share caused by eligible proc perks, never as 20% total DPS;
- a clear winner only when the P50 edge exceeds `max(30 minutes, 3% remaining route P50)` and conservative uncertainty intervals do not overlap.

Otherwise Tier 6 is `unscored` or `close`. Tier 7 compares all remaining encounters, deaths, supplies, first clears, and non-combat points—not dummy DPM.

## Blessing epochs

Track six path picks in two three-pick segments, derived God Tier 1/2, three resets, and complete epoch history. Two matching paths grant that God; one of each grants Balance. Reset recommendations must repay before Dragon after rebuild/setup/failure costs and pass the same permanent-choice margin.

Reject True Equilibrium benchmarks until relic-alignment provenance and the combat resolver agree with the official card. Unsupported mechanics are unknown/partial/scenario-dependent, never ordinary zero.

## Freeze gate

Freeze only after full source reconciliation, legal dependencies, all-option region/relic/Blessing review, 48,000 executable points plus reserve, meaningful skilling/PvM queues, second review of every step over five minutes, and owner approval of irreversible choices.
