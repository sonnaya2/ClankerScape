Prototype audit coverage — design document mapping

Focused Workbench

One dominant stage, narrow context rail, concise inspector/summary rail. Major frames only; nested groups use rules and surface shifts.

Progressive disclosure

Gear advanced filters, passive/set detail, Stats assumptions, Target traits/scenario, Rotation optimizer, run diagnostics and model notes are contextual rather than permanent.

EverSense influence

Rotation and Arch borrow the transport/instrument rail. Invention/results borrow hard tagged headings and data discipline. The RuneScape dark brass/teal material system remains dominant.

Non-goals

No engine math, no generated art, no SaaS cards, no drag-only interaction, no second state store. This is a visual/interaction prototype only.
