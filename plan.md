# ClankerScape Dragon Route

**Target:** 48,000 League Points.  
**Public behavior:** show this route; do not turn it into an editable planner.  
**Current source status:** the checked-in canonical task snapshot has 533 Easy/Medium tasks worth 11,110 points. Exact task-row ordering remains provisional until Hard, Elite, and Master rows are published.

## Working route

### Regions

**Misthalin + Havenhythe → Karamja → Desert → Asgarnia → Anachronia**

- Start in Misthalin + Havenhythe.
- Karamja opens automatically at 50 completed tasks.
- First elective at 175: **Desert**.
- Second elective at 300: **Asgarnia**.
- Third elective at 450: **Anachronia**.

The route deliberately keeps both easy skilling and PvM live so a bad grind, death streak, crowded activity, or awkward requirement does not stall the whole account.

### Relics

1. **Golden Touch**
2. **Animal Wrangler**
3. **Voidwalker**
4. **Crystal Grace**
5. **Production Master**
6. **Rejuvenated** — take **Devout** as the additional earlier-tier relic
7. **Infernal Fire**

Rejuvenated is the current Tier 6 route pick. The route values a complete extra relic across banking, supplies, familiar scaling, travel, skilling, and combat more than Perkfection's narrower Invention compression.

### Combat

**Necromancy is the primary route style.**

Use early City of Um/Necromancy progression while it is cheap and legal. Add **Magic** as the secondary style after Crystal Grace makes the package worthwhile. Prefer first-kill and short mastery clusters over long repeat-kill grinds until the post-450 point push.

### Blessings

1. **Big Boned**
2. **Abyssal Cinders**
3. **Avernic Rampage**
4. **Demon's Mark**
5. **True Equilibrium**
6. **Lord of Light**
7. **Tempered Heart**
8. **Genesis Essence**

`True Equilibrium` remains provisional for combat-value estimates until the combat implementation scales from relic alignments exactly as the official wording does. That caveat does not need to become a configuration control on the public page.

## Phase A — 0 to 50 tasks

Stay dense in **Misthalin + Havenhythe**.

Sweep low-requirement tasks across:

- Thieving;
- Woodcutting;
- Firemaking;
- Fletching;
- Fishing;
- Cooking;
- Mining;
- Smithing;
- Prayer;
- City of Um / early Necromancy;
- low-risk first-kill combat;
- cheap Global counters that complete naturally.

Do not camp a long XP grind just before a relic/XP multiplier. Train to the next useful task threshold, collect the points, then move.

## Phase B — 50 to 175 tasks

**Karamja opens automatically at 50 tasks.** Do one local sweep instead of repeatedly crossing the island.

Cheap Karamja anchors include:

- collect 5 seaweed;
- pick a pineapple;
- fill Luthas' banana crate;
- catch a karambwanji;
- enter Brimhaven Dungeon;
- cross the stepping stones;
- claim one Brimhaven Agility Arena ticket;
- kill a snake;
- defeat a TzHaar;
- catch 25 lobsters on Karamja.

When the combat setup is already reliable, clear **TzTok-Jad** and equip the **Fire cape**. Do not turn the first Karamja visit into a 60-ticket Agility grind or repeated Jad farming unless later task values justify it.

## Phase C — 175 to 300 tasks

Unlock **Desert** first.

Use the region to widen the task pool without committing to one slow progression line:

- batch cheap Desert and Global thresholds by locality;
- use Menaphos work when it overlaps other progression instead of grinding reputation for its own sake;
- keep Necromancy first-kill combat moving;
- process production stacks when several tasks can pay from the same inventory/setup;
- defer high-friction repeat kills and isolated grinds.

The goal of this phase is not maximum Desert completion. It is reaching 300 tasks with stronger account capability and both lanes still usable.

## Phase D — 300 to 450 tasks

Unlock **Asgarnia** second.

This is the infrastructure phase:

- turn on Invention access and useful item/gizmo progression;
- finish efficient Mining/Smithing batches;
- use Production Master to cash stacked production tasks quickly;
- take **General Graardor** first and short Combat Mastery clusters;
- take **Nex** when Defence and gear are already sufficient rather than forcing the requirement early;
- clear leftover Desert tasks during production or machine downtime;
- keep Slayer assignments aligned with task/Blessing/mastery overlap.

Do not mistake Invention infrastructure for a reason to choose Perkfection. Rejuvenated still has to be compared against the whole remaining route, and the current mixed-lane plan prefers the additional Devout package.

## Phase E — 450 tasks to 48,000 points

Unlock **Anachronia** third.

The objective changes after the final region gate. Stop optimizing task count and optimize reliable points per minute.

Prioritize:

- short 80- and 200-point clusters;
- first kills and short mastery steps;
- BGH / Animal Wrangler overlap;
- Slayer/combat overlaps;
- production batches;
- high-value skilling thresholds already close to completion;
- cross-region tasks that became cheap because Desert + Asgarnia + Anachronia are all open.

Keep roughly **4,000 points of easy reserve** until the final Dragon push is locked. The reserve exists so one unexpectedly bad PvM grind or requirement does not force a route rewrite near 48,000.

## Source and freeze rule

The strategy above is the working route and is what the website should display.

The **exact row-by-row task sequence** remains provisional until the canonical Equilibrium task page reconciles to:

- 1,152 unique numeric task IDs;
- 109,380 total League Points;
- all five tiers: Easy, Medium, Hard, Elite, Master;
- no Catalyst/testing rows.

When the remaining task tiers publish, rerun the task ordering inside this route before changing the route itself. Region, relic, or Blessing changes should require a material time saving, not merely a new theoretically stronger option.

## Public-site constraint

ClankerScape is not a configurator.

The public site should contain:

- the fixed phase order;
- the chosen region path;
- the chosen relic line;
- the chosen Blessing line;
- concise caveats only where they change execution.

It should **not** contain task-completion controls, plan dropdowns, editable time estimates, relic/region pickers, local storage, import/export, source dashboards, internal audit terminology, or other controls that ask the user to recreate the route.
