# Planning review

The earlier planning review has been superseded by the implemented adversarial audit in [`docs/final-audit.md`](final-audit.md) and the final contract in [`plan.md`](../plan.md).

The decisive corrections are:

- no Rejuvenated or Perkfection default;
- all 20 relic choices evaluated by remaining-route minutes;
- Perkfection modeled across its full official card without treating 20% proc frequency as 20% total DPS;
- no ordinary relic reset at any stage;
- three Blessing-progression resets stored as epochs;
- no elective-region default from total points;
- final route blocked until the numeric 1,152-task / 109,380-point corpus reconciles;
- imported tasks start unclassified instead of being silently mislabeled as skilling;
- True Equilibrium benchmarks rejected until relic alignment is sourced and the combat resolver is corrected;
- dependency-free static implementation in place of an assumed Vite/React stack.
